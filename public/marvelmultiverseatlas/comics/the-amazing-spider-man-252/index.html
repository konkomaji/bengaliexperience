The Amazing Spider-Man #252 (1984)

The Amazing Spider-Man #252, published in 1984 by Tom DeFalco, Roger Stern · Ron Frenz, is the comic behind Spider-Man 3 (2007). The black suit arrives in the main title — the alien costume that becomes Venom.
