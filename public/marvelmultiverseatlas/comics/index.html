Questions

What comic is a Marvel movie based on?

Most Marvel films adapt a specific comic run rather than a general character history — Avengers: Infinity War and Endgame draw on Jim Starlin's The Infinity Gauntlet (1991), Captain America: Civil War on Mark Millar's Civil War (2006), and Thor: Ragnarok on Planet Hulk (2006). This Atlas maps 94 landmark comics to the films and series that adapted them.

Is this every Marvel comic?

No, and it does not claim to be. Marvel has printed tens of thousands of issues and no complete offline dataset of them exists. This is the source-material spine: 103 landmark issues from 1939 to 2020 — the first appearances that created the characters and the storylines the screen has actually adapted — labelled as a curated selection rather than a catalogue.

Where can I read these comics?

Almost all of them are on Marvel Unlimited, Marvel's own subscription archive, and the major storylines are in print as collected editions. Every comic listed here links out to where it can be read; nothing is hosted or reproduced on this site beyond cover art credited to Comic Vine.
