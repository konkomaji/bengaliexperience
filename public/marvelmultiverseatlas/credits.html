Data sources

Release dates, runtimes, episode counts, box office, reality designations, connection facts — and, in the Comics view, the landmark issues, their cover dates, creators and first appearances — were cross-checked across these.

The Movie Database Every poster image, plus TMDB ids, synopses and the “where to watch” provider listings. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Wikipedia Release dates, runtimes, season and episode counts, directors, studios, budgets and box office. Text available under CC BY-SA. Marvel.com Official announcements, the published in-universe chronology, and confirmation of premiere dates. Marvel Database The Earth-XXXXX reality designations for all 51 universes mapped here. Hosted on Fandom, CC BY-SA. IMDb Cross-checking air dates and episode counts for older television and animation. Box Office Mojo Box Office Mojo Worldwide lifetime gross figures for every theatrical release in the dataset. Rotten Tomatoes Reception context used while verifying recent 2025–2026 releases. JustWatch Powers the “where to watch” streaming-provider listings shown on every title, region-aware. GCD Grand Comics Database Cover dates, issue numbers, writers and artists for the landmark comics in the Comics view. Volunteer-run, openly licensed. Comic Vine Comic Vine Cover artwork for the comics in the Comics view, served at runtime. Covers remain the copyright of Marvel and their respective artists. Marvel Database — Comics First-appearance issues, character debuts and the storylines each film and series adapts. Hosted on Fandom, CC BY-SA.
