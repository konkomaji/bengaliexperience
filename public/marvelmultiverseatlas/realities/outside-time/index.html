Outside Time The Time Variance Authority

Outside Time is the designation for The Time Variance Authority in the Marvel multiverse. 1 screen title is set there, and it takes part in 3 verified crossings with other realities. Not an Earth at all. A bureaucracy sitting outside the flow of time, pruning branched realities into the Void — the junkyard where erased variants go to be eaten by Alioth.
