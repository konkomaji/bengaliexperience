Earth-10005 The Fox Mutant Timeline

Earth-10005 is the designation for The Fox Mutant Timeline in the Marvel multiverse. 8 screen titles are set there, and it takes part in 20 verified crossings with other realities. Twenty years and thirteen films of X-Men, from Bryan Singer's 2000 original through Deadpool 2 — the timeline Deadpool & Wolverine finally canonised as an anchor reality.
