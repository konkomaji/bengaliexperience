Earth-688B Sony's Spider-Man Universe

Earth-688B is the designation for Sony's Spider-Man Universe in the Marvel multiverse. 6 screen titles are set there, and it takes part in 12 verified crossings with other realities. Six films of Spider-Man's rogues without a Spider-Man. Venom, Morbius, Madame Web and Kraven share a reality that brushes the MCU twice and never merges with it.
