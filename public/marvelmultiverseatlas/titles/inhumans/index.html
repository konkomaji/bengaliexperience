Marvel's Inhumans (2017)

Marvel's Inhumans is a 2017 Marvel series, directed by Scott Buck, produced by Marvel Television / ABC Studios. It was released on 29 September 2017 on ABC. Inside the story it is set in roughly 2017; Marvel has never stated the year, so that placement is an estimate rather than canon. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).

The story

After the Royal Family of Inhumans is splintered by a military coup, they barely escape to Hawaii where their surprising interactions with the lush world and humanity around them may prove to not only save them, but Earth itself.
