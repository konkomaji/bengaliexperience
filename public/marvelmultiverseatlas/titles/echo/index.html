The story

Michael is a young man caught in a downward spiral of drug addiction, plagued by insomnia and consumed by guilt over past mistakes. As he struggles to cope with his demons, a mysterious presence begins to haunt him - the ghost of his deceased mother. Unable to distinguish between reality and hallucination, Michael is forced to confront his innermost fears and with this, confront the ghosts of his past.
