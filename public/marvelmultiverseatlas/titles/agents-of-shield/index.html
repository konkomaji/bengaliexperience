Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. (2013)

Marvel's Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. is a 2013 Marvel series, directed by Joss Whedon (dev.), produced by Marvel Television / ABC Studios. It was released on 24 September 2013 on ABC. Inside the story it is set in 2012–2019, a date Marvel's published chronology confirms. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).

The story

Agent Phil Coulson of S.H.I.E.L.D. (Strategic Homeland Intervention, Enforcement and Logistics Division) puts together a team of agents to investigate the new, the strange and the unknown around the globe, protecting the ordinary from the extraordinary.
