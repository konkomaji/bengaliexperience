Marvel's Jessica Jones (2015)

Marvel's Jessica Jones is a 2015 Marvel series, directed by Melissa Rosenberg, produced by Marvel Television / ABC Studios. It was released on 20 November 2015 on Netflix. Inside the story it is set in roughly 2015–2019; Marvel has never stated the year, so that placement is an estimate rather than canon. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).

The story

After a tragic ending to her short-lived super hero stint, Jessica Jones is rebuilding her personal life and career as a detective who gets pulled into cases involving people with extraordinary abilities in New York City.
