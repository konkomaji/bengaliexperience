Doctor Strange (2016)

Doctor Strange is a 2016 Marvel film, directed by Scott Derrickson, produced by Marvel Studios. It was released on 4 November 2016. Inside the story it is set in 2016–2017, a date Marvel's published chronology confirms. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).
