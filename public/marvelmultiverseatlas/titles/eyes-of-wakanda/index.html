Eyes of Wakanda (2025)

Eyes of Wakanda is a 2025 Marvel series, directed by Todd Harris, produced by Marvel Studios Animation / Proximity. It was released on 1 August 2025 on Disney+. Inside the story it is set in 1260 BC – 1896 AD, a date Marvel's published chronology confirms. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).

The story

Follow the adventures of brave Wakandan warriors throughout history in this globe-trotting adventure, where they must carry out dangerous missions to retrieve Vibranium artifacts from the enemies of Wakanda. They are the Hatut Zaraze and this is their story.
