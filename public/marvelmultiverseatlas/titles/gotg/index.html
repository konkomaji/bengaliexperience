Guardians of the Galaxy (2014)

Guardians of the Galaxy is a 2014 Marvel film, directed by James Gunn, produced by Marvel Studios. It was released on 1 August 2014. Inside the story it is set in 2014, a date Marvel's published chronology confirms. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).

The story

Light years from Earth, 26 years after being abducted, Peter Quill finds himself the prime target of a manhunt after discovering an orb wanted by Ronan the Accuser.
