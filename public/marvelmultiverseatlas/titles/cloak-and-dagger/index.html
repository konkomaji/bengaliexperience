Marvel's Cloak & Dagger (2018)

Marvel's Cloak & Dagger is a 2018 Marvel series, directed by Joe Pokaski, produced by Marvel Television / ABC Signature. It was released on 7 June 2018 on Freeform. Inside the story it is set in roughly 2018; Marvel has never stated the year, so that placement is an estimate rather than canon. It belongs to The Sacred Timeline (Earth-199999).
