What movies and shows are in MCU Phase Four?

Phase Four of the Marvel Cinematic Universe covers 18 titles — 7 films and 9 series plus 2 specials and shorts — released between 15 January 2021 and 25 November 2022, as part of the Multiverse Saga. Together they have grossed $5.72 billion worldwide. In release order they are: WandaVision, The Falcon and the Winter Soldier, Loki, Black Widow, What If...?, Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, Eternals, Hawkeye, Spider-Man: No Way Home, Moon Knight, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, Ms. Marvel, Thor: Love and Thunder, I Am Groot, She-Hulk: Attorney at Law, Werewolf by Night, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special.

What is the Multiverse Saga?

The Multiverse Saga is the second overarching MCU story, following the Infinity Saga. It spans Phases Four, Five and Six and takes the multiverse itself as its subject, which is why so many of its titles connect outward to realities the MCU had never touched before.

Why do some dates say estimated?

Marvel rarely puts a year on screen. Where Marvel's own published chronology confirms one, this Atlas marks the date confirmed. Where it does not, the date is marked estimated and is the consensus of press and research rather than canon. 54 titles carry confirmed in-universe dates and 18 are marked as estimates.
