What is the Multiverse Saga?

The Multiverse Saga is the second overarching MCU story, following the Infinity Saga. It spans Phases Four, Five and Six and takes the multiverse itself as its subject, which is why so many of its titles connect outward to realities the MCU had never touched before.

Why do some dates say estimated?

Marvel rarely puts a year on screen. Where Marvel's own published chronology confirms one, this Atlas marks the date confirmed. Where it does not, the date is marked estimated and is the consensus of press and research rather than canon. 54 titles carry confirmed in-universe dates and 18 are marked as estimates.
